\begin{abstract}
Google DeepMind's open-weight Gemma 4 models---spanning edge dense tiers (E2B, E4B at 128K context) and workstation tiers (12B, 26B A4B MoE with $\sim$3.8B active / 25.2B total parameters, and 31B Dense at 256K context) \cite{gemma4}---enable private single-accelerator software engineering (SWE) agents \cite{jimenez2024}. Yet on consumer workstations, agents confront (1) graph-textualization loss under tight KV-cache budgets \cite{repocoder,kgllmbench} and (2) multi-turn limit-cycle thrashing without structured state-space backtracking \cite{yao2023,tot2024}. We study both layers orthogonally in Wayfinder v3.1, combining Hop-Skeletonized Topological Code-Graph Slicing (TCGS), State-Adaptive Discrete Contractive Lyapunov Verification (DCLV) \cite{banach,khalil}, and a sub-millisecond Bellman-Ford \texttt{DPLL(QF\_RDL)} invariant check \cite{nieuwenhuis2006}. Across 45 targets in five Python standard-library packages (1,917 AST nodes, 5,997 edges), TCGS ($k=2$) achieves 54.7\% 2-hop recall vs.\ 18.7\% for BM25 ($p = 7.62 \times 10^{-9}$) and 10.4\% for PageRank RepoMap at 16.69$\times$ compression. On factorial live GPU evaluation with official \texttt{google/gemma-4} weights (\texttt{E2B-it} and \texttt{E4B-it} on 2$\times$ Tesla T4, $N=20$ seven-file Python diagnostic repositories, 100 unit tests, 6-call ceiling; SHA-256 \texttt{acf9de0b44c37be3}), Wayfinder achieves 20.0\% vs.\ 5.0\% BM25 on \texttt{E2B-it} and 60.0\% vs.\ 40.0\% BM25 and 50.0\% fixed-$\lambda_0$ DCLV on \texttt{E4B-it} (paired McNemar $p = 0.092285$ vs.\ BM25, $p = 0.375$ vs.\ Fixed DCLV), with 0.0\% cyclic thrashing and 100.0\% verified \texttt{QF\_RDL} witnesses.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Deploying autonomous repository-repair agents \cite{jimenez2024} on single-accelerator hardware \cite{gemma4} requires both token-efficient graph textualization \cite{kgllmbench} and inference-time state-space backtracking \cite{tot2024}. Lexical retrieval \cite{repocoder} misses cross-file domain contracts when distractor modules share identifier tokens, while unguarded ReAct loops \cite{yao2023} enter 2-cycle attractors ($S_A \leftrightarrow S_B$). Conversely, fixed-ratio contraction ($\lambda_0=0.75$) over-rejects valid single-file intermediate progress on coupled multi-file bugs ($V: 7.5 \to 6.5 > 5.625$).

\section{Topological Code-Graph Slicing (TCGS)}
TCGS parses Python ASTs via \texttt{ast.parse} without executing untrusted code, linking imports, inheritance, and scoped calls bidirectionally (hub cap 24). Breadth-first slicing emits full AST source for hops $d \in \{0,1\}$ and read-only typed interface summaries for hop $d=2$ \cite{kgllmbench}.

\section{State-Adaptive DCLV and \texttt{QF\_RDL} Certificate}
Let observable repository state at turn $t$ be $S_t = \langle \mathcal{F}_t, \mathcal{T}_t, \mathcal{U}_t \rangle \in \mathcal{S}$. Targeting $\mathcal{S}^* = \{S \mid \mathcal{T}=0 \land \mathcal{U}=0\}$ \cite{banach,khalil}, we define $V: \mathcal{S} \to \frac{1}{2}\mathbb{N}_0$ with $(\alpha,\beta,\gamma)=(1.5,1.0,0.5)$ and lattice quantum $\Delta_{\min}=0.5$:
\begin{equation}
V(S_t) = \alpha \mathcal{T}_t + \beta \mathcal{U}_t + \gamma \mathbb{I}(\mathcal{T}_t > 0) |\mathcal{F}_t \Delta \mathcal{F}_0|, \quad \lambda_{\text{eff}}(S_t) = \max\!\left(\lambda_0, 1 - \frac{\Delta_{\min}}{V(S_t)}\right).
\end{equation}

\begin{theorem}[Finite-Step Lattice Convergence and Zero Thrashing]
Under transactional rollback, SHA-256 Taboo $\mathcal{H}_t$, and neutral allowance $B_{\text{neutral}}$, rejected proposals leave $S_t$ invariant, cyclic revisits on $V>0$ equal $0.0\%$, and accepted transitions terminate in at most $K_{\text{adapt}} \le 2V(S_0) + B_{\text{neutral}}$ steps.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[\texttt{QF\_RDL} Witness Soundness \cite{nieuwenhuis2006}]
Encoding contraction, non-negativity, file-blast ($|\tilde{\mathcal{F}} \Delta \mathcal{F}_0| \le B_{\text{files}}$), and prompt-token ($\text{tokens}(\tilde{S}) \le B_{\text{token}}$) bounds as difference inequalities $x_i - x_j \le c_{ij}$, Bellman-Ford relaxation in $O(|\mathcal{X}|\cdot|\mathcal{E}_c|)$ either yields an independently verified witness $\pi$ or extracts a minimal negative-weight cycle.
\end{theorem}
